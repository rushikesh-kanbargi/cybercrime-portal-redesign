# 04 — User Personas

## Citizen
Needs fast, reassuring, understandable help.

## Victim
Needs safety, minimal repeated questioning, evidence preservation, and meaningful updates.

## Business
Needs structured incident reporting, evidence handling, and response guidance.

## Investigator
Needs cases, entities, evidence, relationships, timelines, financial links, and actions.

## Intelligence Analyst
Needs trends, correlations, campaigns, threat signals, and explainability.

## Command Center
Needs national/state situational awareness and operational metrics.

## Institutional Partner
Needs tightly scoped, audited integration and response workflows.
