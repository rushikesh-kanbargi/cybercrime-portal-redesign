# 21 — Roadmap

## P0 — Foundation / Launch Critical
- repository/codebase assessment
- secure identity foundation
- citizen portal shell
- reporting workflow
- emergency financial-fraud guidance flow
- complaint tracking
- secure evidence upload
- basic case management
- audit logging
- accessibility/localization foundation
- suspicious entity checker foundation

## P1 — High Value
- AI-assisted complaint drafting
- save/resume
- incident timeline
- duplicate detection
- entity intelligence
- investigator dashboard
- risk scoring
- notification/communication center
- scam intelligence library

## P2 — Advanced Intelligence
- knowledge graph
- campaign detection
- financial intelligence graph
- community intelligence
- threat reputation engine
- command center
- advanced analytics
- investigator AI copilot

## P3 — Ecosystem / Scale
- authorized bank/telecom/platform integrations
- national threat feeds
- cross-border workflows
- automated disruption workflows with human approval
- advanced multilingual voice
- advanced predictive analytics

## Priority Rule
Prefer: Impact × Feasibility × Safety.

Do not start downstream intelligence features before the underlying data model, permissions, evidence, and audit foundations are stable.
