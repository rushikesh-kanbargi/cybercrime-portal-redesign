# 18 — Integrations

Potential authorized integration classes:
- banks/payment ecosystem
- telecom operators
- social/technology platforms
- domain/hosting providers
- police/agency systems
- cybersecurity agencies
- international coordination systems

## Architecture
Use adapters/interfaces and explicit integration contracts.

Until a real endpoint and authorization exist, label integrations as:
- Mock
- Sandbox
- Proposed
- External Dependency
- Production

Never fake production integration.
