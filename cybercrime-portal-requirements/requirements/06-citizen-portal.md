# 06 — Citizen Portal

## Homepage Priorities
1. Check something suspicious
2. Report cybercrime
3. Money stolen? Act now
4. Track complaint
5. Learn about current scams

## UX Requirements
- mobile-first
- plain language
- progressive disclosure
- clear next-step guidance
- low-bandwidth support
- multilingual-ready
- accessible
- trustworthy visual design

## Safety Assistant
Guide users based on what happened, including hacked accounts, suspicious links, financial fraud, blackmail, impersonation, malware, and immediate safety concerns.
