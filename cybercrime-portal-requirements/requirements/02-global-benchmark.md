# 02 — Global Benchmark

Use this as a research index, not as a claim that every capability exists everywhere.

## Reference Systems
- India — NCRP / I4C: reporting, financial fraud response, suspect/indicator reporting and checking, tracking, awareness
- Singapore — ScamShield: prevention, community intelligence, scam checks, blocking/filtering ecosystem
- USA — FBI IC3: national cybercrime intake, aggregation, intelligence and financial recovery support
- Canada — RCMP / NC3 / CAFC: centralized reporting, intelligence coordination, fraud/cybercrime correlation
- Australia — ReportCyber: streamlined reporting, save/resume, status workflows
- UK — Report Fraud: national fraud reporting, victim support, intelligence and policing integration
- France — PHAROS: specialized online illegal-content reporting and routing
- New Zealand — Police / Netsafe / NCSC: smart routing to the appropriate organization
- EU / Europol — country-specific reporting and cross-border coordination
- INTERPOL — international police coordination and public-private cooperation
