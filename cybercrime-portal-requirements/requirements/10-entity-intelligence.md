# 10 — Entity Intelligence

## Entity Types
Person, organization, phone, email, domain, URL, IP, UPI ID, bank account, wallet, crypto address, social handle, messaging account, application, device, incident, case, transaction, campaign, indicator.

## Public Checker
Allow safe checking of suspicious indicators with privacy-aware results.

## Reputation States
Reported, Under Review, Correlated, Verified, Confirmed, Blocked, Resolved, False Positive, Archived.

A complaint is not proof of guilt. Keep provenance, confidence, timestamps, and source classification.
