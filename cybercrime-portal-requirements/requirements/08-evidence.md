# 08 — Evidence Management

## Evidence Types
Screenshots, recordings, images, videos, emails, chat exports, SMS, audio, documents, receipts, bank statements, URLs, and other lawful artifacts.

## Controls
- original preservation
- cryptographic hashing
- metadata capture
- chain of custody
- immutable audit events
- role-based access
- secure object storage
- derived copies separate from originals
- legal hold / retention support where required

## Timeline
Build a visual incident timeline and distinguish exact, approximate, user-entered, and inferred timestamps.
