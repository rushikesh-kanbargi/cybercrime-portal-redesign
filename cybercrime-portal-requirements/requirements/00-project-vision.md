# 00 — Project Vision

## Vision
Create a world-class Indian cybercrime ecosystem that acts as a single intelligent front door for cybercrime reporting, prevention, victim assistance, investigation, intelligence, coordination, disruption, and recovery.

## Core Loop
Detect → Check → Protect → Report → Correlate → Investigate → Disrupt → Recover → Learn

## Primary Audiences
- Citizens
- Victims
- Businesses and organizations
- Investigators and police
- Cyber analysts
- Authorized financial institutions
- Authorized telecom/platform partners
- Government and international coordination teams

## Strategic Differentiator
Move beyond complaint management into an intelligence-driven response platform where validated incidents can improve detection, correlation, campaign identification, prevention, and operational response.
