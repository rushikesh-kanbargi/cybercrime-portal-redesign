    About CCube
    
  
    A faster path from "my money is gone" to a report the cyber cell can act on.
    
  
    
    
    The argument
    
    
    MHA's own instructions list seven data points as enough for police
    to begin the bank request. The form that exists asks for roughly forty-five fields across eight
    steps before it will accept anything. Reporting a suspicious call takes three taps; reporting an
    actual loss takes forty-five fields. Severity is inverted against usability.

    How it behaves
    
    
    Sample data, held in your browser. Nothing is sent to any bank, police or
    government system, and no account is frozen — police examine a report and make that request themselves.
    The only outbound link is the phone call to 1930.
