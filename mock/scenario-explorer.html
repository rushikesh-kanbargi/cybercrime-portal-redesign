Cybercrime Scenario Atlas

MHA’s own instructions say seven data points are enough for police to begin the bank request. Its reporting form asks for ~45 fields across 8 steps before it will accept anything. Twenty-eight scenarios, and exactly what data each one honestly needs.

Fields marked ★ are the seven data points MHA’s own CFCFRMS instructions list as enough for police to start the bank request — mobile, bank/wallet, account or UPI ID, transaction ID, transaction date, card number (card fraud only) and a screenshot. Amount is not among them; we collect it because it drives triage. Everything else can wait until the request is moving. Full catalogue in docs/specs/01-scenarios.md; data model in docs/specs/02-schema.md.
