Step 3 of 4

The money trail

One number matters more than everything else: the transaction ID. Banks find the money with it. Here is where yours is hiding.

🔍 Google Pay
  1. Open Google Pay and tap your photo, top right
  2. Tap See all activity and open the payment
  3. Scroll down — the long number under UPI transaction ID is it
🔍 PhonePe
  1. Tap History at the bottom
  2. Open the payment, then tap View details
  3. Copy the UTR — that is the same thing as a transaction ID
🔍 Paytm
  1. Tap Balance & History
  2. Open the payment and tap View full details
  3. The UPI Ref. No. is the number we need
✉ Only have the bank SMS?

That is enough. Paste it below — the reference number is usually the last long number in the message, after the words Ref, UTR or txn.

Illustration of a common pattern, not your case.

The money does not sit still. It is split and forwarded through borrowed accounts within minutes. Every hop makes it harder to hold.

A hold has to reach the account before the money leaves it. That is the entire race — and it is why a report that takes forty-five fields loses it.

Reporting late is still worth it. Even after the money is gone, your report is what links the account to everyone else it took money from.

Things this does not do

It does not freeze accounts. It does not contact your bank, the police, or any government system. It does not file a complaint. No form here is submitted anywhere — the only outbound link in the entire prototype is the phone call to 1930, which is genuinely the right first move.

Field lists are drawn from MHA's Citizen Manual v10, dated 2019. The live portal may have changed since. We would rather say that than let you assume otherwise.
